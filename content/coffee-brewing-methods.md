# Coffee brewing methods

This is a **sub-article** — it lives under its parent, [Coffee](/coffee), and shows up nested in the sidebar.

## Common methods

1. **Espresso** — pressurised hot water through finely-ground beans
2. **Pour over** — slow, hands-on extraction (e.g. V60, Chemex)
3. **French press** — steeped, full-bodied, easy
4. **Moka pot** — stovetop, strong and rich
5. **Cold brew** — steeped cold for 12–24h, smooth
