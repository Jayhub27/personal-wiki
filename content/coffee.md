# Coffee ☕

Coffee is a **brewed drink** made from roasted coffee beans, native to tropical Africa and now grown around the world. It's one of the most consumed beverages globally.

## Types of beans

- **Arabica** — sweeter, more complex, ~60% of world production
- **Robusta** — stronger, more caffeine, more bitter
