# Coffee history

Another **sub-article** under [Coffee](/coffee). This one links out to a real encyclopedia article for reference.

## Timeline highlights

- **~850 CE** — Legend of Kaldi the goatherd in Ethiopia, who noticed his goats "dancing" after eating coffee cherries
- **15th century** — Coffee cultivated and traded in the Arabian Peninsula
- **17th century** — Coffeehouses spread through Europe
- **Now** — Global industry worth hundreds of billions
