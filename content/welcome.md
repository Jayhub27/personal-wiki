This is your **personal library**, draped in an animated aether. It's built around a few simple ideas:

- **Articles & sub-articles** — nest related pages under a parent using the *Parent* field.
- **Wiki links** — link pages by typing `[[Page Title]]`. If a page doesn't exist yet, it shows as a red link and you can create it.
- **Media** — upload **images, video and audio** from the [Media library](/uploads) and embed them.
- **References** — attach a link to a real encyclopedia article for further reading.

## Try it right now

Click the links below to see the system in action — they link to pages you can create:

- [[A place to start]] — your first article
- [[My first sub-article]] — nest one under it

## How to write

Write in **Markdown**. A quick cheat-sheet:

```markdown
# Heading
## Sub-heading

**bold** and *italic*, and `inline code`

[[Another Wiki Page]]      <- wiki link
![](/uploads/image.png)    <- embed an image
<video controls src="/uploads/video.mp4"></video>
<audio controls src="/uploads/audio.mp3"></audio>

- a list
- of things
```

## Keyboard shortcuts

All the buttons in the editor toolbar let you **insert** formatting around selected text. No need to memorize Markdown.
